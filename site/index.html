Made for people with more than one account

Quota at a glance

Gemini and Claude quotas for every account, the 5-hour and weekly windows side by side, with reset times.

Knows where to go next

When your account runs low, the one with the most quota left is marked, ready to switch to.

Switch safely

Confirms first, closes Antigravity and the agy CLI, verifies the new sign-in, and rolls back if anything fails.

Add accounts in the browser

A regular Google sign-in page. No signing out of Antigravity, and the account you already use is picked up.

Feels native

A Liquid Glass popover on macOS and a compact tray window on Windows, in light and dark.

Private by design

Tokens stay in your system keychain and only ever go to Google. No server, no analytics, no telemetry.

Switching, start to finish

Pick an account, confirm, and AgyOrbit does the rest: it checks the account's authorization, closes Antigravity, signs it in to the new account and opens it again.

Install

  1. Download

    Get the .dmg for macOS or the installer for Windows. Antigravity must already be installed.

  2. Open it the first time

    Builds aren't code-signed yet. On macOS, right-click AgyOrbit and choose Open, or run: 

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AgyOrbit.app

    On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway in SmartScreen.

  3. Click the planet

    AgyOrbit appears in the menu bar. Your current Antigravity account is already there; add more with Add account.

Questions

Is this safe for my accounts?

AgyOrbit only changes which of your own accounts the official Antigravity app uses. It signs in through Google's normal page, doesn't proxy model traffic or share accounts, and talks to nothing but Google. Please use it within Google's terms.

Do I have to sign out of Antigravity to add an account?

No. Adding an account happens in your browser while Antigravity keeps running.

What does AgyOrbit store?

One refresh token per account in your system keychain, and the account's name, email and plan in a local file. Access tokens live only in memory. The security notes have the details.

Does it work on Windows?

Windows builds are a beta: they build and pass checks in CI but haven't been verified on a real installation yet. Reports are very welcome.

Why does it need Antigravity installed?

AgyOrbit signs accounts in with Antigravity's own OAuth client, read from your local installation, so the sign-in is exactly what Antigravity itself would store.
